TIDE

Privacy policy

Tide has no servers, no ads and no tracking.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Tide is made by one person, Luis Carrasco. It has no servers, no Tide accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in Tide is sent to me.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, goals and settings. They're stored on your iPhone, encrypted with a key kept in your Keychain.
  • Your Sign in with Apple identity. Apple gives Tide an ID for you; it stays on the phone and is only used to keep your data yours.
  • Photos you take of things you own, and their values.
  • Your OpenAI API key, Plaid keys or SimpleFIN access, if you add them. They're kept in the Keychain.

What Tide sends, and to whom

Only to services you choose to connect, using your own accounts, plus a few public data sources:

  • SimpleFIN Bridge or Plaid. If you connect your banks, Tide reads your balances and transactions from them directly. Their own privacy policies cover what they do.
  • OpenAI. Only if you add your own API key. Tide sends transaction descriptions and amounts so they can be filed, and your questions when you use Ask. It's billed to your OpenAI account. OpenAI's privacy policy covers what they do with requests.
  • Apple Intelligence. Filing purchases and other smart features run on your iPhone.
  • Yahoo Finance. To show what your investments are worth today and how they've moved, Tide asks Yahoo Finance for prices of the ticker symbols you hold (like "VOO"). Only the symbols are sent, never your name, accounts or amounts.
  • Public data. Home price indexes from the FHFA, used-car prices from the BLS, car details from NHTSA (using a VIN you enter), car photos from Wikipedia, and logos from merchants' own websites. None of these requests include anything about you.

Tips

If you leave a tip, it goes through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the payment; I don't see who you are.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything on this iPhone deletes all of Tide's data and signs you out. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Tide isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. It doesn't collect anything from anyone.
