TIDE

Support

Tide is a calm, simple money app for iPhone, made by one person, Luis Carrasco.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open a support request (free GitHub account needed). I read everything.

Connecting your banks

Tide reads your accounts through SimpleFIN Bridge, a separate service (about $15 a year) at bridge.simplefin.org. Sign up there, connect your banks, make a setup token, and paste it in Tide (Settings › Banks). If you have your own Plaid developer account, you can use its keys instead.

Only about three months of history

SimpleFIN Bridge shares roughly the last 90 days. Tide builds your budget from that, and history grows from there.

A purchase is in the wrong category

Tap it and pick the right one. Turn on "Do this for all" to fix every purchase from that merchant at once.

Smarter filing and Ask

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Tide uses it on the device. You can also add your own OpenAI API key in Settings; it's billed to your OpenAI account.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase everything on this iPhone. There's no Tide server, so that's everything.
